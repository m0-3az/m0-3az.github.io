01 — About

Systems I can run, and break, and fix again

I'm a cybersecurity student at the Arab Open University working in parallel as a Linux system administrator. The two tracks feed each other: the admin work gives me hands-on experience with the systems an attacker would actually target, and the security studies give me the vocabulary and reasoning to reason about them.

My day-to-day is servers, users and the unglamorous work between them — disk and permissions that make sense, services that restart when they should, backups that were actually tested, and logs worth reading. I care most about the parts that only fail at 3am.

I'm comfortable working from a terminal, and I document as I go. If a fix isn't written down, it hasn't really been fixed.
